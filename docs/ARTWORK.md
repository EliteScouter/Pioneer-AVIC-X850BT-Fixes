# Background artwork

Built-in imagegen edit of the project's charcoal/red music background.

Prompt:

> Remove ONLY the small red STi logo at right below center from this firmware
> music background, filling that area with seamless matching dark charcoal
> brushed-metal texture. Preserve all other design elements, the charcoal
> brushed metal background, the thin red top and bottom lines, and the same
> aspect ratio. No lettering, no logos, no watermark, no icons or controls
> anywhere. This is the neutral public-release version of this exact background.

The selected image was converted with System.Drawing to 680×483, 24-bit BMP.
The conversion matches stock channel precision and clamps every channel to at
least 8. Original source photos, vehicle artwork and personal branding are not
included. `background-preview.png` is a lossless format conversion of the
actual BMP pixels supplied to the firmware builder.
